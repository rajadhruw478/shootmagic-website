BEFORE YOU INSTALL

System Requirements

Compatibility guidance for this product. Confirm your exact setup with Shootmagic before purchase.

  • Operating system: Windows 10 and Windows 11.
  • Adobe Photoshop: Adobe Photoshop CS3–CS6 and all CC versions supported, as specified by Shootmagic. The latest CC version is preferred for best compatibility and features. Photoshop is required separately.
  • Memory & storage: Use a workstation suitable for your Photoshop version and album PSD sizes. Keep space for the software, selected template packs, photo originals and exported PSD/JPG files. The full template collection is 150 GB+.
  • Internet & activation: Internet access is needed to obtain setup and packs. Contact the team for license activation requirements and update delivery.
GETTING STARTED

Installation Procedure

  1. Send your chosen PC / duration plan and Photoshop version to Shootmagic on WhatsApp.
  2. Obtain the official setup, activation instructions and the asset / template pack links from the team.
  3. Install the software using the supplied Windows installer. Choose a writable location for downloaded templates and creative packs.
  4. Complete activation or the available trial process, then choose your Photoshop version at launch.
  5. Select your template and output folders in the software. Add photos and test one compatible template before starting a full album.
  6. Create a small test album, check the PSD/JPG output and PDF preview, and keep your original photos backed up.
