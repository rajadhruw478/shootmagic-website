YOUR QUESTIONS, ANSWERED

Before you get started.

What do I need to use Album Maxx?

Album Maxx is a Windows desktop application that works with Adobe Photoshop. Photoshop is required separately. Ask us to confirm support for your installed Photoshop version before purchasing.

Can I try it before buying?

Yes—use Request Trial to enquire about for the current trial setup and its limits. Test it with your Photoshop version and your PSD templates before choosing a plan.

Can I use my own PSD templates?

Compatible PSD templates use normal pixel photo placeholders named H1, H2… for horizontal photos and V1, V2… for vertical photos. Photo placement uses clipping masks. Contact us if your templates need preparation.

Which album sizes can I use?

The workflow supports 12×36 and 14×40 template categories. Adaptive 12×36 designs can be used in the supported 14×40 workflow. Use your own matching templates or ask about available packs.

Are Photoshop and template packs included?

Photoshop is not included. Ask for the exact template and asset packs included with your chosen offer; pack availability and license terms are confirmed before purchase.

How do purchase and activation work?

Choose a plan and send a purchase enquiry once our contact details are updated. We confirm the price, payment instructions and license terms, then provide setup and activation details. This website does not collect payments or issue licenses automatically.

Need a little guidance?

Tell us about your Photoshop version, templates and editing setup. We can help you choose the right workflow.
